Fruity Glow · Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

In short: Fruity Glow has no accounts and we run no servers. Your scores and settings stay on your phone.

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads.

Who we are

Fruity Glow ("the app") is a fruit-merging puzzle game made by Gorill Aballz ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

What the app stores on your device

Your best score, settings (sound and vibration), number of games played and daily power-up use are saved only on your device. We do not receive this data, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: an ad when a new game starts (unless you buy Remove ads) and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch for extra power-ups. To show ads, limit repeats, measure performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • how you interact with ads and the app
  • crash and diagnostic information

This data is sent to Google and handled under Google's policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only remembers on your device that the purchase was made, so it can be restored from Settings.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone other than Google for the advertising and purchase features described above. Data the app sends is encrypted in transit.

Children

Fruity Glow is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can play offline, reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings, buy Remove ads to stop the ads between games, or uninstall the app to delete everything it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
